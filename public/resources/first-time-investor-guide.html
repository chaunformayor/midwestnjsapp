First-Time Investor Onboarding Guide

Everything you need to know before and after your first St. Louis investment property — from goals to your first rent check.
1

Define Your Investment Goals

Before looking at a single property, get clear on what you want your real estate portfolio to accomplish. The answer shapes every decision that follows — which market, which strategy, which financing, how hands-on you want to be.

Ask yourself:

  • Am I optimizing for monthly cash flow (income now) or long-term equity (wealth later)?
  • How much capital do I have available, and how much do I want tied up long-term?
  • What's my timeline — am I building toward financial independence in 5 years, or supplementing income over 20?
  • How involved do I want to be in decisions, and how much do I want to delegate?
Most of our investors target both: cash flow to validate the investment month to month, and equity growth as the long-term wealth engine. The St. Louis market supports both goals simultaneously.
2

Know Your Financing Options

How you finance your first deal determines how much capital you need, your monthly cash flow, and how quickly you can scale. The three most common options for our investors:

OptionBest ForKey Requirements
Conventional Investment Loan Strong W-2 income, good credit, first deal 20–25% down, 700+ credit, DTI under 45%
DSCR Loan Most Popular Self-employed, scaling investors, multiple properties No personal income verification. Property cash flow qualifies. 20–25% down.
Cash / Hard Money → Refinance (BRRRR) Distressed properties, recycling capital Cash or hard money to acquire and renovate, then DSCR refi after ARV established
We can connect you with DSCR lenders we work with regularly in the St. Louis market. Ask your account manager for introductions.

Our core focus is North County — where the math works best for buy-and-hold investors who want real cash flow, not just appreciation bets.

4

Your First Deal — What to Expect

Once we identify a deal that matches your goals, here's the typical sequence:

  • Deal presentation: We send you a full underwriting package — purchase price, rehab scope, ARV, projected rent, cash flow, and return analysis.
  • Decision window: You typically have 48–72 hours to decide. Good deals move quickly.
  • Contract and earnest money: We handle the purchase agreement. You wire earnest money (typically $1,000–$2,500) to hold the property.
  • Financing: Apply for financing immediately if using a loan. Cash buyers proceed to closing timeline.
  • Inspection and due diligence: 10–14 days to complete inspection and review title.
  • Closing: Most deals close within 30 days. You'll wire your down payment and closing costs.
  • Rehab (if applicable): We manage the renovation. Typical timeline is 4–8 weeks depending on scope.
  • Lease-up: We market, screen, and place a tenant. Typical vacancy from rehab completion is 2–3 weeks.
  • First rent check: Usually within 60–90 days of closing.
5

What Property Management Looks Like

Once your property is rented, our management team handles everything:

  • Rent collection — collected monthly, disbursed to you net of management fee and expenses
  • Maintenance — tenant requests routed to our maintenance coordinator; small repairs handled immediately, larger repairs reviewed with you
  • Lease renewals — we handle re-leasing, rent increases, and lease renewals
  • Monthly statements — income, expenses, and net cash flow emailed monthly
  • Annual review — we review your portfolio performance and discuss the next deal
Your investor portal gives you real-time visibility into your properties, documents, and deal history. You don't need to call us to know what's happening — but we're always available when you do.
6

Building Toward Deal Two (and Beyond)

The goal of deal one is not just to own a property — it's to prove the process, build confidence, and position yourself for deal two.

If you use the BRRRR strategy, your refinance proceeds fund the next acquisition. If you used conventional financing, your cash flow and equity growth build the down payment for the next deal. Most of our investors are under contract on their second property within 12–18 months of closing their first.

A 5-deal portfolio acquired over 5 years, with conservative St. Louis performance numbers, can generate:

  • $1,400–$2,000/month in net passive cash flow
  • $150,000–$250,000 in equity
  • A fully managed, income-producing asset base that works regardless of what your primary career does
