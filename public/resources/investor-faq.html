Investor FAQ

Answers to the most common questions from new and prospective investors.
Getting Started
What does Midwest Investor Services actually do?
We are a St. Louis-based real estate investment firm that finds, acquires, renovates, and manages rental properties for investors. We handle the full lifecycle — from deal sourcing and underwriting through renovation management and ongoing property management — so investors can build a cash-flowing portfolio without managing day-to-day operations.
What is the minimum investment to get started?
Most of our single-family deals require $60,000–$100,000 in available capital to cover the down payment, rehab, closing costs, and initial reserves. The exact amount depends on the deal structure. BRRRR deals often allow you to recycle much of that capital after refinancing. We'll talk through your specific capital position on our first call.
Do I need to be a Missouri resident to invest?
No. We work with investors across the country. Our local team handles everything on the ground — you can manage your portfolio remotely through your investor portal and monthly reporting.
How do I get started?
Submit an investor inquiry on our website. We'll schedule a call to understand your goals, capital position, and timeline. If it's a fit, we'll send over a current deal or add you to our deal flow list so you see opportunities as they become available.
Deals & Markets
What markets and neighborhoods do you focus on?
We focus exclusively on St. Louis metro — primarily St. Louis County (Florissant, Jennings, Normandy corridor), North County, and select South City neighborhoods. These submarkets offer strong rental demand, affordable acquisition prices, and cash flow that is difficult to find in most other major metros.
What types of properties do you acquire?
Primarily single-family homes and small multifamily (2–4 units). Most of our inventory is 3-bedroom, 1–2 bath brick homes built between 1940 and 1975. These are the backbone of the St. Louis rental market and the properties that Section 8 tenants and working-class families actively compete for.
What returns can I realistically expect?
On stabilized buy-and-hold deals, we target 6–10% cash-on-cash return in year one. BRRRR deals, when executed correctly, can return most or all of your initial capital while leaving you with a cash-flowing asset. Actual returns vary by deal — we'll share the full underwriting on any deal we present before you commit.
These are projections based on our typical deal parameters. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
Do you do Section 8 / Housing Choice Voucher rentals?
Yes. Many of our properties are Section 8 eligible and we actively work with voucher holders. HCV tenants generally have lower turnover, government-guaranteed rent, and regular inspection oversight — which can improve long-term performance. We'll discuss whether Section 8 makes sense for a specific property during underwriting.
Property Management
Who manages the properties after acquisition?
We provide full-service property management as part of our investor relationship. This includes tenant placement, rent collection, maintenance coordination, lease renewals, and monthly reporting. You have visibility into everything through your investor portal.
How are tenants screened?
We run a full background check, credit check, eviction history, and income verification on every applicant. We look for tenants with steady income (minimum 3x monthly rent), no recent evictions, and a history of on-time payments. Section 8 tenants go through an additional voucher verification step.
What are your management fees?
Our standard management fee is 8–10% of collected rent, depending on the property and agreement terms. Leasing fees, maintenance coordination, and other fees are outlined in the management agreement. We'll walk through the full fee structure during onboarding.
What happens if a tenant stops paying?
We follow Missouri's landlord-tenant law for the eviction process. We initiate notice immediately after a missed payment, file for eviction if payment is not received, and coordinate with counsel through lockout if necessary. We keep you informed at every step. Evictions are rare for properly screened tenants but we handle them efficiently when they occur.
Your Investor Account
How do I track my portfolio performance?
You have access to your investor portal at any time. It shows your active properties, deal history, documents, and portfolio resources. We also provide monthly statements with rent collected, expenses, and net distributions.
Who do I contact if I have a question?
Your primary point of contact is your account manager. You can reach us by email at info@midwestinvestorservices.com or through the contact form in your portal. For urgent property matters, our property management team is reachable by phone during business hours.
