Rental Property Due Diligence Checklist

Complete before making an offer. Use for single-family and small multifamily acquisitions.
Property Address:  _______________________________________ Date:  _________________

1 · Property Basics

Confirm property type (SFR / duplex / 3–4 unit)
Verify beds / baths / square footage against tax records
Note year built and construction type (brick / frame / other)
Confirm lot size and zoning classification
Check for any unpermitted additions or conversions
Verify property is in flood zone or not (FEMA map)

2 · Physical Inspection

Roof condition and estimated remaining life
HVAC systems (age, condition, last service)
Water heater (age, capacity)
Electrical panel (age, breakers, amperage)
Plumbing (type, visible leaks, water pressure)
Foundation and basement (cracks, moisture, grading)
Windows (condition, age, type — single vs double pane)
Exterior (siding, trim, gutters, downspouts)
Kitchen condition (cabinets, appliances, counters)
Bathrooms (fixtures, tile, exhaust fans)
Flooring throughout (type, condition)
Evidence of pest activity or mold
Inspector name & company
Estimated deferred maintenance
Estimated rehab budget (if applicable)

3 · Financial Review

Request 12 months of rent rolls / rent history
Request 12 months of utility and expense records
Confirm current rent vs. market rent (comp analysis)
Verify property taxes (current year + any pending reassessment)
Get current insurance quote for the property
Check HOA fees if applicable
Gross monthly rent
Monthly taxes + insurance
Monthly management fee (est. 8–10%)
Vacancy allowance (est. 8%)
CapEx reserve (est. 5–8%)
Net monthly cash flow (before mortgage)

4 · Title & Legal

Order title search — confirm clear title
Check for outstanding liens (mechanics, tax, HOA)
Verify ownership chain and legal description
Confirm no active code violations or open permits
Review any existing leases for terms and tenant rights
Confirm occupancy status (vacant / tenant-occupied)

5 · Neighborhood & Market

Pull 6 months of comparable rentals within 0.5 miles
Pull 6 months of sold comps for ARV validation
Drive the neighborhood at different times of day
Check school ratings (GreatSchools or DESE data)
Note proximity to employment centers, transit, and retail
Confirm Section 8 / HCV voucher acceptance and payment standard

6 · Go / No-Go Decision

Asking price
All-in cost (purchase + rehab + closing)
After-repair value (ARV)
Projected monthly net cash flow
Cash-on-cash return (annual / cash invested)
